# ShopEasy — Official Store Policy

## 1. Returns & Exchanges

### Return Eligibility
- Items are eligible for return within **30 days** of the delivery date.
- The item must be in its original, unused condition with all original packaging and tags attached.
- Items that have been used, damaged by the customer, or missing parts are not eligible for return.
- Digital products, gift cards, and perishable goods are **not returnable**.

### Exchange Policy
- Exchanges are accepted for a different size or colour of the same product within 30 days of delivery.
- If the replacement item has a higher price, the customer must pay the difference.
- If the replacement item has a lower price, ShopEasy will issue a store credit for the difference.

### How to Initiate a Return or Exchange
1. Contact ShopEasy support via chat, email, or phone.
2. Provide your order ID and the reason for return.
3. The support agent will schedule a pickup slot at your address.
4. Pack the item securely and hand it to the courier at the scheduled time.
5. Once the returned item is received and inspected, the refund or exchange will be processed within 5–7 business days.

### Return Shipping
- ShopEasy covers the return shipping cost for defective or incorrectly shipped items.
- For change-of-mind returns, a flat return shipping fee of **$5.00** will be deducted from the refund amount.

---

## 2. Refunds

### Refund Eligibility
- Refunds are issued for:
  - Items returned within the 30-day return window.
  - Orders cancelled before shipment.
  - Items that arrived damaged, defective, or significantly different from the product description.

### Refund Method
- Refunds are returned to the **original payment method** used at checkout.
- If the original payment method is no longer available (e.g. expired card), a store credit will be issued instead.

### Refund Timeline
- Once the returned item is received and inspected: **5–7 business days** to process.
- After processing, the refund appears in your account within **3–5 additional business days** depending on your bank.
- Order cancellations before shipment are refunded within **1–2 business days**.

### Partial Refunds
- If only part of an order is returned, only the cost of the returned item(s) will be refunded, minus any applicable return shipping fee.

---

## 3. Shipping & Delivery

### Delivery Estimates
- **Standard shipping**: 5–7 business days.
- **Express shipping**: 2–3 business days (additional charge applies).
- **Same-day delivery**: Available in select cities; order must be placed before 11:00 AM local time.

### Shipping Fees
- Free standard shipping on orders over **$50**.
- Orders under $50: standard shipping fee of **$4.99**.
- Express shipping: **$12.99** flat rate.

### Order Processing
- Orders are processed within **1 business day** of being placed.
- Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed on the next business day.

### Tracking
- A tracking link is emailed to you as soon as your order ships.
- You can also retrieve your tracking status by asking the support agent your order ID.
- Tracking updates can take **up to 24 hours** to reflect after an item is picked up by the carrier.

### Carrier Delays
- ShopEasy is not responsible for delays caused by the carrier, weather events, or customs.
- If your order is more than 3 business days late, contact support and we will investigate with the carrier.

## 4. Privacy Policy

### Data We Collect
ShopEasy collects the following information when you create an account or place an order:
- Full name and email address.
- Shipping address and phone number.
- Payment information (stored in encrypted form; full card numbers are never stored in plain text).
- Order history and support ticket history.

### How We Use Your Data
- To process and fulfil your orders.
- To send order confirmations, shipping updates, and support communications.
- To improve our products, services, and customer experience.
- To comply with legal obligations.

### Data Sharing
- ShopEasy does **not** sell your personal data to third parties.
- We share data only with:
  - Shipping carriers (name and address, to deliver your order).
  - Payment processors (encrypted payment data, to charge your card).
  - Legal authorities, only when required by law.

### Data Retention
- Your account data is retained for as long as your account is active.
- After account deletion, data is purged within **30 days**, except for records required for legal or tax compliance (kept for 7 years).

### Your Rights
You have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you.
- Request correction of inaccurate data.
- Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements).
- Opt out of marketing communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact support@shopeasy.com.

### Cookies
- ShopEasy uses essential cookies for session management and cart persistence.
- Analytics cookies are only placed with your explicit consent.
- You can manage cookie preferences in your account settings.

## 5. Payments & Security

### Accepted Payment Methods
- Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover.
- PayPal and Apple Pay.
- ShopEasy store credit.

### Payment Security
- All transactions are encrypted using **TLS 1.3**.
- Payment data is handled by a PCI-DSS compliant payment processor.
- ShopEasy never stores your full credit card number or CVV on its servers.

### Failed Payments
- If a payment fails at checkout, no charge is made to your account.
- If a payment fails after an order is confirmed, ShopEasy will contact you within 24 hours to resolve the issue.

### Fraud Protection
- Suspicious orders may be held for manual review.
- You will be contacted by email if your order is under review.
- ShopEasy will never ask for your full card number, CVV, or PIN via chat or email.

## 6. Order Management

### Cancellation Policy
- Orders can be cancelled **free of charge** before they are shipped.
- Once an order has shipped, it cannot be cancelled; you must initiate a return after delivery.
- To cancel an order, contact support as soon as possible with your order ID.

### Modifying an Order
- Address changes are possible only if the order has not yet been dispatched.
- Product changes (colour, size, quantity) are not possible after an order is confirmed; you would need to cancel and reorder.

### Order Issues
- Wrong item received: Contact support with your order ID and a photo of the item. ShopEasy will arrange a free return and send the correct item.
- Missing item: Contact support within 7 days of delivery. ShopEasy will investigate and reship the missing item or issue a refund.
- Damaged item: Contact support within 48 hours of delivery with photo evidence. ShopEasy will issue a full refund or replacement.

## 7. Customer Support & Escalation

### Support Channels
- **Live chat**: Available 24/7 via the ShopEasy website and app.
- **Email**: support@shopeasy.com — responses within 24 hours.
- **Phone**: +1-800-SHOPEASY — available Mon–Fri, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM EST.

### Escalation to a Human Agent
- If the virtual assistant cannot resolve your issue, you can request to speak with a human agent at any time.
- Human agents are available Mon–Fri, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM EST.
- Outside business hours, a ticket will be created and a human agent will follow up the next business day.

### Support Ticket
- Every issue is logged as a support ticket with a unique ticket ID.
- You will receive an email confirmation with your ticket ID after contacting support.
- You can reference your ticket ID in future conversations to get faster follow-up.
